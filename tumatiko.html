In a
nutshell

Tumatiko is an event ticketing platform I built from the ground up. Organisers sell tickets, buyers pay with M-Pesa, gate teams scan QR codes at the door, and getting a ticket takes about as long as making tea.

The origin
story

Organisers pour their hearts into their events, and the behind-the-scenes work (tracking sales, sorting payments, managing the team, checking people in) deserves to feel just as easy. Buyers just want to grab a ticket and get excited about the night.

So I set out to build one place where all of it just works, for everyone on both sides of the ticket.

Built
to this

The song on repeat while I made it.

WHAT IT DOES

  1. 01
    FOR BUYERS

    Zero accounts

    Pick a ticket, pay with M-Pesa or card, and you’re in. You can follow organisers or save events without signing up, because buyers deserve peace.

  2. 02
    FOR ORGANISERS

    Mission control

    Create events with ticket tiers, group deals and instalment plans (because December is expensive). Run multi-city tours with venue maps, watch sales roll in, request payouts, export reports and bring your team along with roles and tasks.

  3. 03
    AT THE GATE

    The bouncer’s best friend

    A QR scanner that runs right in the phone browser. Point, scan, and the gate team knows in a second whether you’re good to go.

  4. 04
    BEHIND THE CURTAIN

    Admin console

    Role-based permissions, audit logs, support tools and a resend button for when someone swears they never got the email.

THE TRICKY BITS

  1. 01

    No payment left behind

    Payment confirmations don’t always show up on time, and sometimes they don’t show up at all. Tumatiko double-checks with the payment provider on its own, so if you paid, you get your ticket.

  2. 02

    Many ways to pay, one system

    Full price, group deals for the whole squad, or instalments spread over a few weeks. Each works differently, but they all end up as the same kind of order, so sales, refunds and reports stay simple.

  3. 03

    Everyone sees just their part

    Buyers, organiser teams and platform admins each get exactly the tools and data they need, and nothing that isn’t theirs. One login flow, three very different experiences.

  4. 04

    Built to keep standing

    About 200 automated tests guard the important stuff, like payments, tickets and permissions, so new features never quietly break old ones.
