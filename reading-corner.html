A reading site that's part library and part scrapbook. It's built around an interactive bookshelf that always opens on the book I'm reading right now.

WHAT'S INSIDE

  1. 01

    The bookshelf

    The home page is an interactive shelf you can filter by section and genre: owned, reading, TBR, finished, book club and series.

  2. 02

    Now reading

    It always opens on the book I’m reading right now, with a progress bar underneath.

  3. 03

    Book sheets

    Each book has its own detail sheet with progress notes, saved quotes, a review, the song on repeat while I read it, and a map.
